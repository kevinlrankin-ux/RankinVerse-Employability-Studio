# Workforce-Forming Assignment Template

## Purpose

This template defines the standard structure for assignments created through the Electrical Trades Program OS.

The assignment should teach a technical skill while also embedding safety, leadership, executive-function support, documentation, and reflection.

## Materials and Tools

## Safety Lens

What safety habit or safety check is embedded in this task?

Prompt:

Before beginning, identify one risk, one prevention step, and one point where you should pause and check your work.

## Leadership Lens

What leadership behavior is embedded in this task?

Prompt:

Identify your responsibility in this task. If working with others, identify who is responsible for setup, checking, documentation, and cleanup.

## Executive-Function Scaffold

What planning, sequencing, time management, or self-check support is embedded?

Prompt:

List the first three steps before starting. After finishing, compare your plan to what actually happened.

## Documentation Habit
