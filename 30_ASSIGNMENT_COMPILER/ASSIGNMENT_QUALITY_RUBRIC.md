# Assignment Quality Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric helps evaluate whether a generated or drafted assignment is good enough to move toward classroom use.

It is for instructor review.

## Rating Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Strong / ready or nearly ready |
| 2 | Usable with revision |
| 1 | Weak / needs significant revision |
| 0 | Missing or not applicable |

## Rubric

| Category | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical Skill | Skill is clear and central to the task. | Skill is present but could be clearer. | Skill is vague or weakly connected. | No clear skill. |
| Task Clarity | Student can understand what to do. | Task mostly clear but needs edits. | Task is confusing. | No usable task. |
| Safety Integration | Safety is natural and task-specific. | Safety is present but general. | Safety feels added on. | No safety lens. |
| Leadership Integration | Leadership behavior is observable and useful. | Leadership is present but could be sharper. | Leadership feels vague. | No leadership lens. |
| Executive-Function Support | Scaffold helps students plan, sequence, check, or finish. | Scaffold is useful but could be simpler. | Scaffold feels like busywork. | No scaffold. |
| Documentation Habit | Student output is clear and inspectable. | Documentation exists but needs refinement. | Documentation is vague. | No documentation habit. |
| Rubric Alignment | Criteria match the task well. | Criteria mostly match. | Criteria are weakly connected. | No rubric. |
| Reflection | Prompt is short, useful, and connected. | Prompt is usable but generic. | Prompt is vague or too long. | No reflection. |
| Classroom Fit | Feels realistic for the instructor's classroom. | Usable with classroom edits. | Feels generic. | Not usable. |
| Governance Fit | Review, sharing, and data boundaries are clear. | Boundaries mostly clear. | Boundaries need clarification. | Boundary missing. |

## Decision Guide

| Total Score | Suggested Decision |
|---|---|
| 25-30 | Ready or nearly ready for classroom use after final review. |
| 18-24 | Good draft; revise before use. |
| 10-17 | Rework significantly. |
| 0-9 | Do not use yet. |

## Review Notes

- What should be kept?
- What should be revised?
- What should be removed?
- What would make this feel more like the actual classroom?
