# Teacher Review Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist helps the instructor review assignment drafts before they are used in class.

Generated materials remain drafts until the instructor reviews them.

## Review Questions

### 1. Classroom Fit

- Does this feel like my actual classroom?
- Is the task realistic for my students?
- Is the assignment clear enough to use without extra explanation?

### 2. Technical Skill

- Is the technical skill clearly named?
- Does the task actually practice that skill?
- Are the expectations concrete?

### 3. Safety

- Is the safety lens appropriate for the task?
- Does safety appear naturally inside the work?
- Is there a clear check, pause, or prevention habit?

### 4. Leadership

- Is the leadership prompt practical?
- Does it ask for an observable behavior?
- Does it support responsibility, communication, quality, or shared work?

### 5. Executive Function

- Does the scaffold help students start, sequence, check, or finish?
- Is the scaffold small enough to be useful?
- Does it avoid feeling like extra busywork?

### 6. Documentation

- Does the assignment say what students must record?
- Would another person be able to inspect or understand the work?
- Is the documentation habit connected to trade practice?

### 7. Rubric

- Are the rubric criteria fair?
- Are they observable?
- Do they match the actual assignment?

### 8. Reflection

- Is the reflection prompt short and useful?
- Does it help students notice quality, safety, planning, or learning?

### 9. Governance

- Does the assignment require no student personal data to generate?
- Is it teacher-reviewed before use?
- Is the sharing category correct?
- Is the tool boundary clear?

## Review Decision

Choose one:

- Ready for classroom use
- Needs revision
- Hold for later
- Archive
