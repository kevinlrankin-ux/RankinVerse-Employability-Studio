# Wire Applications Vertical Slice

## Purpose

This is the third course-specific proof slice for the Electrical Trades Program OS.

The goal is to show how the OS supports hands-on wiring practice, procedural accuracy, documentation, and workmanship.

## Course

Wire Applications

## Course Role

Wire Applications builds applied installation skill, conductor handling, termination practice, circuit construction, neatness, safety, and procedural accuracy.

## Why This Course Matters

Wire Applications is where students practice the physical habits that connect theory, code, safety, and employability.

This course helps the OS support:

- conductor identification
- stripping and preparation
- termination quality
- neatness
- procedure-following
- tool control
- safety habits
- visible workmanship
- documentation

## Starter Unit

Basic Conductor Preparation and Termination Practice

## Technical Skill

Students prepare conductors and complete basic terminations with attention to conductor condition, tool use, connection quality, and neatness.

## Student Task

Students will identify conductors, prepare the conductor ends, complete assigned terminations, inspect their work, and document one quality check.

## Materials and Tools

- conductors
- wire strippers
- terminal device or practice board
- screwdriver or approved tool
- label or marker as needed
- assignment sheet
- teacher-provided setup instructions

## Safety Lens

Tool readiness and verification before action.

Student prompt:

Before beginning, check that the correct tool is selected and that the conductor or device condition is ready for the task. Identify one step that should be verified before calling the work complete.

## Leadership Lens

Handoff readiness.

Student prompt:

Prepare your work so another person could inspect it and understand what was done. Your work should be clear, neat, and ready for review.

## Executive-Function Scaffold

Materials check and completion check.

Student prompt:

Before starting, confirm the required materials and tools. Before submitting, check your work against the completion criteria.

## Documentation Habit

Students record:

- conductor type or assigned conductor
- tool used
- termination completed
- one quality check performed
- one issue corrected or avoided

## Rubric Criteria

| Criteria | Evidence of Success |
|---|---|
| Preparation | Conductors are prepared correctly for the task. |
| Termination Quality | Connection is secure, neat, and appropriate for the practice task. |
| Tool Use | Student uses the correct tool with control. |
| Safety Awareness | Student identifies a tool or verification concern. |
| Handoff Readiness | Work can be inspected and understood by another person. |
| Documentation | Student records key details and one quality check. |

## Reflection Prompt

What detail most affected the quality of your termination? What will you check first next time?

## Teacher Notes

Possible common errors:

- over-stripping or under-stripping
- damaging conductor strands
- loose termination
- poor tool selection
- messy layout
- not checking final connection quality

Teacher reminders:

- tool choice matters
- neatness supports inspection
- connection quality is part of safety and professionalism
- students should slow down during preparation and final check
