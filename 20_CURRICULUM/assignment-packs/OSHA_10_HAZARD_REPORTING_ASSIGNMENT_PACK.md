## Technical Skill

Students identify a workplace hazard, explain why it matters, and describe an appropriate response or reporting step.

## Student Task

Students will:

1. Read a teacher-provided workplace scenario.
2. Identify the hazard.
3. Explain the possible consequence.
4. Describe the appropriate safe action or reporting step.
5. Explain who else could be affected.
6. Identify one prevention idea.

## Materials and Tools

- teacher-provided scenario
- approved OSHA-10hr notes or reference
- assignment sheet
- pencil or digital workspace

## Safety Lens

Hazard prediction and reporting awareness.

Students identify the hazard, possible consequence, and appropriate response step.

## Leadership Lens

Care for shared safety.

Students explain how their response protects more than only themselves.

## Executive-Function Scaffold

Stop point.

Students identify where a worker should pause before continuing.

## Documentation Habit

Students record:

- hazard identified
- possible consequence
- immediate safe action
- reporting or response step
- who may be affected
- one prevention idea

## Rubric

| Criteria | Evidence of Success |
|---|---|
| Hazard Recognition | Student identifies the hazard accurately. |
| Risk Explanation | Student explains why the hazard matters. |
| Response Step | Student identifies a reasonable action or reporting step. |
| Shared Safety | Student recognizes impact on others. |
| Documentation | Student records hazard, consequence, and response clearly. |

## Reflection Prompt

Why is reporting or speaking up part of safety?

What can happen if a hazard is ignored?

## Teacher Notes

Keep the tone clear, direct, and serious.

Watch for students naming only an injury instead of identifying the hazard or skipping the response step.
