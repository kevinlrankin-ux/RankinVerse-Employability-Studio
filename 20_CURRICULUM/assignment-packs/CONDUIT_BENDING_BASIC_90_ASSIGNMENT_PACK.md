## Technical Skill

Students will plan, mark, bend, and check a basic 90-degree conduit bend.

## Student Task

You will prepare a piece of conduit for a basic 90-degree bend.

You will:

1. Review the assigned measurement.
2. Mark the conduit.
3. Confirm tool position and work area readiness.
4. Complete the bend.
5. Check the bend for accuracy and workmanship.
6. Record your result and reflection.

## Materials and Tools

- conduit
- hand bender
- tape measure
- marker or pencil
- work surface
- assignment sheet
- teacher-provided measurement criteria

## Safety Lens

Pre-task hazard prediction and tool readiness check.

Before beginning, identify one thing that could go wrong and one action that helps prevent it.

Check that the bender and work area are ready before starting.

## Leadership Lens

Quality check partner.

Before final review, ask a partner to check one measurement, bend angle, or mark location.

Be prepared to explain what was checked and what changed.

## Executive-Function Scaffold

Start plan and sequence check.

Before starting, list your first three steps.

After completing the bend, compare your plan to what actually happened.

## Documentation Habit

Record the following:

- assigned measurement
- mark location
- final result
- one correction or adjustment made
- one quality check performed

## Rubric

| Criteria | Evidence of Success |
|---|---|
| Measurement Accuracy | Mark and bend location match assignment expectations. |
| Bend Quality | Bend is clean, controlled, and close to the required angle. |
| Safety Awareness | Student identifies a realistic risk and prevention step. |
| Planning | Student lists steps before beginning and follows a clear sequence. |
| Peer Check | Student participates in a partner quality check. |
| Documentation | Student records measurement, result, and reflection clearly. |

## Reflection Prompt

What part of the bend required the most attention?

What would you check sooner next time?

## Teacher Notes

Common errors to watch for:

- rushing the measurement
- marking from the wrong reference point
- incorrect bender orientation
- overbending or underbending
- skipping the final quality check

Teacher reminders:

- measure before bending
- confirm tool orientation
- use controlled pressure
- check the result before calling the task complete
