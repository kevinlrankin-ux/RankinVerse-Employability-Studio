## Technical Skill

Students will prepare conductors and complete basic terminations with attention to conductor condition, tool use, connection quality, and neatness.

## Student Task

You will identify conductors, prepare conductor ends, complete assigned terminations, inspect your work, and document one quality check.

You will:

1. Review the assigned termination task.
2. Confirm the correct conductor and tool.
3. Prepare the conductor end.
4. Complete the termination.
5. Inspect the connection quality.
6. Record one quality check and one correction or avoided issue.

## Materials and Tools

- conductors
- wire strippers
- terminal device or practice board
- screwdriver or approved tool
- label or marker as needed
- assignment sheet
- teacher-provided setup instructions

## Safety Lens

Tool readiness and verification before action.

Before beginning, check that the correct tool is selected and that the conductor or device condition is ready for the task.

Identify one step that should be verified before calling the work complete.

## Leadership Lens

Handoff readiness.

Prepare your work so another person could inspect it and understand what was done.

Your work should be clear, neat, and ready for review.

## Executive-Function Scaffold

Materials check and completion check.

Before starting, confirm the required materials and tools.

Before submitting, check your work against the completion criteria.

## Documentation Habit

Record the following:

- conductor type or assigned conductor
- tool used
- termination completed
- one quality check performed
- one issue corrected or avoided

## Rubric

| Criteria | Evidence of Success |
|---|---|
| Preparation | Conductors are prepared correctly for the task. |
| Termination Quality | Connection is secure, neat, and appropriate for the practice task. |
| Tool Use | Student uses the correct tool with control. |
| Safety Awareness | Student identifies a tool or verification concern. |
| Handoff Readiness | Work can be inspected and understood by another person. |
| Documentation | Student records key details and one quality check. |

## Reflection Prompt

What detail most affected the quality of your termination?

What will you check first next time?

## Teacher Notes

Common errors to watch for:

- over-stripping or under-stripping
- damaging conductor strands
- loose termination
- poor tool selection
- messy layout
- not checking final connection quality

Teacher reminders:

- tool choice matters
- neatness supports inspection
- connection quality is part of safety and professionalism
- students should slow down during preparation and final check
